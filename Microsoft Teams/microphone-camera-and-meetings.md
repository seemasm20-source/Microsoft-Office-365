
# Microsoft Teams Microphone, Camera and Meeting Troubleshooting

**Support Level:** Tier 1  
**Service:** Microsoft Teams / Windows

## Objective

Troubleshoot microphone, camera, and audio/video problems in Microsoft Teams meetings.

## 1. Diagnose Audio and Video Issues

1. **Check Teams device settings:** Open Teams → **Settings and more (…) → Settings → Devices**. Confirm the correct microphone, speaker, and camera are selected. Menu names may vary by Teams version.
2. **Make a test call:** If available, go to **Settings → Devices → Make a test call**. Check whether the microphone, speakers, and camera work.
3. **Check microphone permissions:** In Windows 11, open **Settings → Privacy & security → Microphone**. Enable microphone access and allow desktop apps to access the microphone.
4. **Check camera permissions:** Open **Settings → Privacy & security → Camera**. Enable camera access and allow desktop apps to access the camera.
5. **Check the physical device:** Confirm the headset or camera is connected, not muted by a hardware switch, and not being exclusively used by another application.
6. **Check Device Manager:** Right-click Start → **Device Manager**. Expand **Audio inputs and outputs**, **Sound, video and game controllers**, and **Cameras**. Look for warning icons or disabled devices.

## 2. Fix — Microphone or Camera Not Working Only in Meetings

1. Join the meeting and check the microphone and camera buttons in the meeting toolbar.
2. Confirm the correct microphone and camera are selected in the meeting's device settings.
3. Check whether the meeting organiser or an administrator has restricted microphone or camera use. Ask the organiser to review meeting permissions if necessary; an organiser cannot always unmute another participant directly.
4. Leave the meeting and rejoin.
5. If the issue continues, test the device in Teams settings or another permitted application to identify whether the problem is Teams-specific.

## 3. Fix — Microphone or Camera Not Detected

1. Disconnect and reconnect the headset or camera, or try another USB port.
2. Close applications that may be using the device.
3. Restart Teams and test again.
4. In Device Manager, check whether the device is disabled or has a driver warning.
5. If necessary, follow your organisation's approved driver update or escalation process.

**Caution:** Do not uninstall drivers or change device settings without following your organisation's support procedure.

## 4. Verification

- [ ] Correct microphone, speaker, and camera selected.
- [ ] Microphone and speaker pass the available test.
- [ ] Camera preview works.
- [ ] User can hear and be heard in a test meeting.
- [ ] The issue is resolved or documented for escalation.

## Troubleshooting Notes

- If multiple users experience the same issue, check Teams service health and meeting policies.
- If only one user is affected, check device selection, permissions, hardware connections, and drivers.
- Record the affected device, error message, troubleshooting steps, and outcome in the support ticket.
